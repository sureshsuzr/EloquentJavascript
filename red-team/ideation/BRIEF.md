# Ideation brief: what should this founder build?

Date: 3 Nov 2026. Every ideation agent reads this file.

## Objective (first principles)
Maximise the founder's **probability-adjusted wealth over 10 years**, subject to:
1. **Replace a ~$500k/yr income reasonably quickly** (or keep it while de-risking, then jump).
2. **High job satisfaction**: work that uses and stretches him; he was energised by building Fulfilio from nothing.
3. Ambition is high. Ideas are **not constrained to his current skills**: he built a logistics business without logistics background through relentless determination and curiosity, using the inputs he had.

## The founder
- **Education/background:** computer scientist, statistician; ex-BCG; reasonably prestigious academic and work background, credible to raise capital (VC, PE, family offices, strategics).
- **Career:** former CEO of LocalAgentFinder (real-estate agent comparison marketplace, Australia). Founded **Fulfilio** (warehouse/fulfilment platform, acquired by Australia Post); its real edge was a *physical arbitrage*: consolidating spare warehouse space into local delivery hubs. Currently a **senior finance and operations executive (CFO-type) in a SaaS company**, earning ~$500k. Heavy hands-on AI user (agents, LLMs).
- **Location:** Melbourne, Australia. Networks plausibly include: Australian tech/SaaS, ex-BCG alumni, Australia Post/logistics, real estate agency industry, investors from prior exits.
- **No restraint clauses** from prior businesses. Employment-contract duties to his current employer still apply (IP, outside interests, confidentiality).
- Personal risk appetite so far expressed: willing to put roughly $0.75–1.2M of personal capital at risk; no personal guarantees preferred.

## What earlier red-team rounds learned (don't repeat these mistakes; don't simply re-propose these ideas)
Files: `red-team/SYNTHESIS.md`, `red-team/round2/SYNTHESIS.md`, `red-team/round3/SYNTHESIS.md`, `red-team/grocery/SYNTHESIS.md`. Read them briefly.
- **AI-native PM agency (operator):** ops-heavy, licensing-gated, needs 2,500–3,100 properties for a $500k draw; AI cost-out is commoditised by vendors within 12–24 months.
- **Rent-roll pricing engine (software/data):** thin market (250–900 deals/yr); best captured as an *investor* using the engine (hybrid ~A$3.0–3.4M 7-yr EV, floor ~A$1.5M). Currently the leading candidate.
- **Grocery aggregation via merchants:** per-order economics negative, incumbents (DoorDash/Uber) own demand and couriers; a long-tail catalogue/data layer survives as a smaller option.
- **Patterns:** the founder's edges show up in **pricing/underwriting under uncertainty, capital allocation, data layers sold to whoever owns demand or capital, and physical-asset arbitrage**. Ops-heavy density businesses and thin niches did badly. Staying employed ≈ A$2.0–2.4M 7-yr after-tax wealth (with employer equity); this is the bar.
