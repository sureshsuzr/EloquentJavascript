# Red-team protocol (shared by every agent)

1. Read `red-team/grocery/BRIEF.md` in full. You understand the objective: beat a ~$500k tech-executive income on wealth creation and job satisfaction with a grocery aggregation business that uses local merchants as dark stores, with shelf-scan inventory software and a universal SKU catalogue.
2. Work **independently**. You may read `red-team/round3/SYNTHESIS.md` for context on the founder's alternative path, but do not read other grocery reports. Stay in your own lane; your value is a view the others won't have.
3. Be adversarial but constructive: try to kill the idea in your domain, then say what would have to be true for it to survive and how to make it survive.
4. Verify numbers. Use web search where it helps (company filings, investor letters, ABS, ACCC grocery inquiry, IBISWorld summaries, trade press such as Inside Retail, The Grocer, Grocery Dive, TechCrunch). Use AU sources first, then global. Label every figure **[verified: source]**, **[estimate: reasoning]** or **[unverified]**. Don't invent citations. If a fact in the brief looks wrong or stale, say so and give the correction.
5. Today is 2 November 2026. Flag anything time-sensitive.
6. Write your report to `red-team/grocery/reports/<NN>-<slug>.md` (path given in your role) using exactly this structure:

```
# <Role title>: red-team report
## Verdict (3–5 lines: survives / survives-if / fatal, and the single biggest issue)
## Verdict on the business model (which version, if any, is the business, from your lens)
## Attacks on the thesis (only in your domain; evidence, severity High/Med/Low)
## Things the brief gets wrong, misses or understates
## Numbers checked (table: claim | finding | status/source)
## Improvements (concrete changes to the plan, ranked by impact)
## Tests & kill criteria (cheap experiments, metric, threshold, timeframe)
## Answers to the brief's questions in my domain
## Questions I'd hand to other specialists
```

7. Length 1,500–3,000 words. Dense, specific, no filler or generic startup advice. Australian English, AUD (convert foreign figures).
8. When done, reply with only the 5 most important findings (one line each) plus the report path.
