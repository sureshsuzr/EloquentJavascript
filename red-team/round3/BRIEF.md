# Round 3 brief: the software / data business

## Context
Rounds 1–2 (`red-team/SYNTHESIS.md`, `red-team/round2/SYNTHESIS.md`) concluded that an owned PM operator needs ~2,500–3,100 properties for a $500k draw, carries $1M+ personal capital risk and is mostly operational work. The founder's decisive edges were found to be **analytical**, not operational:
1. **Pricing rent-roll acquisitions on modelled retention** (a 5-point retention error ≈ $100k on a $2M roll; incumbents price on rule-of-thumb multiples, roughly 2.8–3.5x management income in Melbourne 2026).
2. **Maintenance cost prediction** from agencies' invoice/job histories (the one data asset generic PM-software AI can't commoditise).
3. Arrears early warning during tenancy; workflow/agent architecture.

Round 1 also noted that "first-mover duopoly" dynamics fit **tools sold to agents**, not operators. The founder (computer scientist, statistician, CFO; ex-BCG; ex-CEO of LocalAgentFinder; founded Fulfilio, acquired by Australia Post) currently earns ~$500k as a tech executive in Melbourne and wants something that beats that on **wealth creation and job satisfaction**.

## The idea to evaluate
A B2B software/data company built on those edges, without holding an estate agent licence or managing property. Candidate products (the agents should judge which, if any, is the business):
- **A. Rent-roll intelligence:** due-diligence and valuation engine that ingests a target's PropertyMe/Console/Rex export (ledger, tenancies, arrears, maintenance, landlord data) and outputs modelled retention, fee quality, risk flags and a retention-adjusted price. Customers: acquiring agencies and franchise groups, rent-roll brokers, banks lending against rolls, PE/roll-up funds, vendors preparing for sale.
- **B. Portfolio health / retention monitoring:** ongoing landlord-churn and book-health analytics for agencies, franchise HQs and lenders (covenant monitoring).
- **C. Maintenance cost intelligence:** cost prediction, benchmarking and pricing for PM agencies, landlord insurers, maintenance platforms, and any future maintenance-plan or warranty products.
- **D. Agentic DD / integration tooling:** AI that does roll due diligence and post-acquisition integration (trust-account reconciliation, compliance backlog, landlord communication).
- Expansion: NZ, UK (Dwelly-style roll-ups, letting-agency M&A), US PM M&A.

## Questions this round must answer
1. Who pays, how much, how often, and is the market big enough to beat a $500k salary plus equity (income and exit value)?
2. Can the founder get the data (system-of-record access, agency consent, privacy), and do the models actually beat rule-of-thumb multiples with the data available?
3. Who already does this (valuers, brokers, Cotality, PropertyMe, Real Estate Dynamics-style benchmarking, US/UK analogues), and what stops PropertyMe or Cotality from building it?
4. Which product is the wedge, and which is the moat?
5. Can it start part-time alongside the current job (employment contract, IP, conflicts)? What capital and timeline?
6. How does it compare with rounds 1–2 (operator, capital partner) on risk-adjusted wealth and job satisfaction?
7. What is the smallest test that proves or kills it within ~90 days?
