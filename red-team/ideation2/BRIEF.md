# Ideation round 2 brief: outcome-based finance × Australia's resources and renewable-energy advantages

Date: 3 Nov 2026. Every agent reads this file.

## Why this round exists
The first ideation round (`red-team/ideation/SYNTHESIS.md`) converged on SME roll-ups (distribution holdco, vertical-software holdco). The founder rejected that, rightly: the space is crowded (search funds, MBAs, laid-off executives, PE and AI-roll-up funds), so it suffers adverse selection or rising entry prices, and it doesn't use the AI shift as anything but a cost lever. The scoring was also biased: it benchmarked against cash salary, used a risk-adjusted value that punished upside, and three of ten lenses were acquisition-centric by design. **Don't recommend generic SME roll-ups or ETA in this round.**

## Revised objective (founder's words, interpreted)
1. **Build equity value** over 10 years in a business that **generates cash**. The founder can keep his own salary low and reinvest; cash in pocket is not the priority.
2. **High probability of a positive outcome; avoid complete failure.** Prefer businesses with a floor (contracted cash flows, hard assets, recoverable capital, optionality) over binary venture bets. Upside still matters.
3. Replace the ~$500k income *in value terms* (equity growth plus modest salary), not necessarily in cash.
4. Job satisfaction and ambition: he is relentlessly curious and determined; he built Fulfilio (logistics) without a logistics background.

## Where to look
**Outcome-based finance:** capital whose return or repayment depends on **measured outcomes** (energy saved or generated, asset performance and availability, emissions avoided, production delivered, rehabilitation achieved, yield, uptime), including:
- performance contracts and "as-a-service" models;
- revenue floors and performance guarantees/insurance;
- royalties and streams;
- savings-share and pay-for-performance structures;
- warranty-backed finance;
- tax-incentive monetisation tied to production.

The founder's edge is pricing these outcomes: statistics (measurement and verification, forecasting, variance), CFO structuring, and AI/data to monitor at scale.

**Australia's advantages** that a Melbourne founder can plausibly access:
- critical minerals and mining (lithium, rare earths, nickel, copper, iron ore and green iron);
- world-leading rooftop solar and home batteries (Cheaper Home Batteries Program from Jul 2025);
- grid-scale storage and renewables build-out (CIS tenders, REZs, Victoria's offshore wind and SEC);
- agriculture and water markets; carbon (ACCUs, Safeguard Mechanism) and the Nature Repair Market;
- Future Made in Australia incentives (Critical Minerals Production Tax Incentive, Hydrogen Production Tax Incentive);
- proximity to Japan, Korea and Southeast Asia;
- deep superannuation capital seeking infrastructure and transition assets;
- mine rehabilitation liabilities.

Location matters: prefer ideas where being in Australia is an advantage, not a handicap.
