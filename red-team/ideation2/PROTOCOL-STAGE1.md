# Round-2 stage-1 protocol: idea generation (independent)

1. Read `red-team/ideation2/BRIEF.md`; skim `red-team/ideation/SYNTHESIS.md` for context. Don't read other round-2 reports.
2. Generate many ideas through your lens; keep the **best 4** (at least one bold). No generic SME roll-ups.
3. Score each on this common rubric:

| Field | What to give |
|---|---|
| One-liner | What it is; who pays; what outcome is priced |
| Why now (2026–28) | Policy, market or technology shift |
| Why Australia / why Melbourne | Advantage of location; any handicap |
| Unfair inputs | What this founder brings (stats, CFO, CS/AI, marketplace, logistics); gaps and who must fill them (co-founder profile) |
| Market & model | Size, pricing, unit economics, comparables (verified where possible) |
| Capital & structure | Equity, debt, fund, project finance, insurer capacity; founder ownership |
| Floor and failure mode | What's left if it goes wrong (assets, contracts, recoverable capital); P(total loss) |
| Outcome distribution (10 yrs) | P(total loss), P(modest), P(good), P(great), with founder equity value in A$; EV, median, and **P(positive outcome)**, defined as ending ahead of staying employed (~A$3.0–3.5M over 10 yrs) |
| Cash generation | When it becomes cash-positive; can it fund growth from its own cash? |
| AI leverage | How AI/data makes the outcome measurable or priceable; does the idea get stronger as models improve? |
| Job satisfaction | What the work looks like |
| Key risks | Top 3 |
| 90-day test | Cheapest proof or kill, with thresholds; can it start while employed? |

4. End with your top pick and a ranked list.
5. Write to `red-team/ideation2/stage1/<NN>-<slug>.md` (2,000–3,500 words). Write early; update as you go. Don't commit.
6. Reply with only: top pick (one line), the four ideas (one line each with EV and P(positive)), and the file path.
