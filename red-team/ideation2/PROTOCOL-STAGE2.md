# Stage 2 protocol: head-to-head evaluation

1. Read `red-team/ideation2/BRIEF.md`, `red-team/ideation2/SHORTLIST.md`, and the stage-1 source files for each candidate. You may read the earlier SYNTHESIS files. Don't read other stage-2 reports.
2. Evaluate **every candidate (B0, B1, S1–S8)** through your assigned lens. Verify key claims with web search where your lens needs it (≤20 searches). Label figures [verified]/[estimate]/[unverified].
3. Score on the round-2 objective: equity value, **P(positive)** (ending ahead of staying), **P(total loss)**, floor, and cash generation, not cash salary. Use **one consistent set of assumptions** across candidates (state them at the top): tax, dilution, founder salary, discount for time, and the stay-employed baseline over 10 years.
4. Output structure:
```
# <Lens>: stage-2 evaluation
## Assumptions
## Scores (table: B0, B1, S1–S8 × your criteria, with a 1–10 overall score from your lens)
## Candidate notes (3–8 lines each; what your lens sees that the stage-1 agent missed)
## Promotions/demotions (anything from the "not shortlisted" list that should be in, or any merger of candidates)
## Ranking and recommendation (top 3, and the one you'd choose)
## Sequencing (what he should do first while employed, and how candidates combine)
```
5. Write to `red-team/ideation2/stage2/<NN>-<slug>.md`. 2,000–3,500 words. Write early; update as you go. Don't commit.
6. When done, reply with only: your ranking of B0, B1, S1–S8 (one line each with your score), your one choice, and the file path.
