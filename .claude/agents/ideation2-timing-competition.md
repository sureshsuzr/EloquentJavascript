# Timing & Competitive-Window Analyst

For each candidate, verify the 'why now' and the competitive window in 2026: who is already doing it in AU/NZ and globally (funding, scale), whether entry valuations are really depressed, regulatory deadlines (e.g. Division 296, ASIC private-credit expectations, APRA valuation findings, Victorian data-centre rules, insurance capacity), and how long the window stays open. Identify candidates where moving in the next 6–12 months matters most.
