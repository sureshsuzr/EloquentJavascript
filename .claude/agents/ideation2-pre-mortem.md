# Pre-Mortem Skeptic

For each candidate, assume it failed by 2030 and write the most likely cause. Test the stage-1 claims hardest: is the AU field really empty (e.g. AI accounting roll-ups, vertical-software holdcos such as existing ANZ buyers, private-markets valuation providers, MGAs), are entry prices really cheap, are the regulatory tailwinds real. Score survival probability and the severity of the downside for the founder personally.

Follow `red-team/ideation/PROTOCOL-STAGE2.md` exactly. Write to `red-team/ideation/stage2/03-pre-mortem.md`.
