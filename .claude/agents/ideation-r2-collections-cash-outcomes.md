# Cash, Collections & Working-Capital Outcomes

Your lens (Track A): The most measurable outcomes in finance are cash: collections (DSO reduction, cash collected), payments (early-pay discounts captured, errors and fraud avoided), and working capital. Evaluate an outcome-priced AR/AP service as the wedge for the full finance function, and whether the data it generates enables an adjacent financing business (invoice finance, supply-chain finance, revenue-based finance) underwritten with the founder's statistics edge. Comparables: Tesorio, Upflow, Chaser, HighRadius, Butn, Marmalade, Waddle (acquired by Xero), Prospa, Moula, OnDeck. Regulatory: payments licensing changes (AU payments reforms 2025–26), ACL for credit, collections conduct (ACCC/ASIC guidelines), Privacy.
